1. Goal and Definition of Done

Goal: One complete freeway loop that a stranger wants to drive again immediately. The cute cab and articulated trailer steer via captured mouse. The road is populated with recognizable drivers. Merging onto a new route is an expressive skill check yielding a Mario flag-style micro-celebration. Police enforcement and road friction are bound strictly to money and notoriety—never arbitrary RNG punishment.

Slice Acceptance Gate (5 Non-Negotiable Conditions)

Thresholds are locked before testing starts and cannot be altered retrospectively. Evaluated cold with 5 unassisted test subjects:

  1. Intuitive Handling: 5 strangers play cold with zero instructions. At least 4 of 5 execute a deliberate lane change within 60 seconds.
  2. Retention Pull: At least 4 of 5 initiate a second run voluntarily without prompting.
  3. Merge Legibility: 0 of 5 testers describe a highway merge as "unfair" in post-play debriefs.
  4. Driver Recognition: At least 3 of 5 recognize the same recurring AI vehicle or stop character on their second run unprompted.
  5. Systemic Fairness: During police stops of other cars, 100% of 200 automated runs preserve an accessible gap around the incident (test AT-19).

Explicitly Out of Slice Scope: Zones 2 through 8, dark/illicit cargo routes, MySQL personality database scraping, mod frameworks, the beat-em-up brawler subsystem, and full Papers, Please-grade border inspections. All preserved as post-slice goals.

3. The Player Loop

Stop 1 (talk to staff, accept job, review route card, pay repairs)
   → on-ramp → MERGE GATE 1 (skill evaluation & flag celebration)
   → freeway (traffic psychology, patrol cruisers, route choice at interchange)
   → [if Route B selected] MERGE GATE 2 onto Route B (flag celebration)
   → off-ramp → camera fade → Stop 2 (delivery payout, customer memory check)

In this vertical slice, "return" equates to an immediate subsequent Stop 1 → Stop 2 haul. The fade back bypasses the empty reverse drive at zero cost. Full reverse-direction road geometry will be layered later.

The Trailer is the Toy

Tactile articulation, soft wells, and mouse drag make steering inherently engaging even in an empty lane.

Traffic is Inhabited

Vehicles are not anonymous dynamic obstacles; they are expressively driven by named characters with social memory.

Legible Consequence

4. Core Driving Model

4.1 Screen & World Coordinate System

  • The cab is anchored vertically at ~75% of screen height (start), featuring a ±0.5 cab-length forward/aft elastic margin: creeping forward under hard throttle and squatting under heavy braking.
  • Lateral displacement is real: the truck crosses discrete lane bands.
  • Highway lanes, ambient scenery, and traffic scroll downward matching the cab's true forward velocity.
  • Coordinates remain segment-local: wrap scroll offsets modulo segment length; pool traffic objects instead of allocating dynamically while driving.

4.2 Steering: Soft Wells with Speed Sensitivity

Mouse capture uses Pointer Lock. The cursor's angle θ around an invisible pivot on the cab translates directly to front wheel rotation. Distance from center adjusts effort/damping, never angle.

Cursor θ (from 12:00)Steering Wheel AngleFront-Wheel Angle (δ)Haptic Well Profile
0°0°0°Soft Magnetic Center
±20°±90°±3.5°Fine adjustment boundary
±40°±360° (1 turn)±14°Medium resistance well
±70°±540° (1.5 turns)±21°Firm resistance well
±115°±720° (2 turns)±28°Heavy resistance well
±150° (clamped)±900° (2.5 turns)±35°Lock well (breakaway flick required)
  • Speed-Sensitive Lock Clamping: Dynamic wheel limit formula: Allowed Rotation = 900° × (1 − 0.75 × speed / top_speed). Front wheel max clamp: 35° × (1 − 0.75 × speed / top_speed). At top speed, max steering is clamped to 8.75° (225° wheel rotation) to preserve highway stability, relaxing to full 35° authority at crawling speeds.
  • Self-Centering: Neutral well pull scales with velocity: 2°/s at rest up to 240°/s at cruising speed (start). Returning mouse to 12:00 snaps wheel from max speed lock (225°) down to <5° front angle in ~0.4s.
  • Slew Limiter: Wheel rotation capped at 720°/s (start). Lock release requires high-velocity mouse flick so jittery hands never slip.
  • HUD: An overlaid translucent steering wheel with a gripping hand icon communicates angular displacement clearly.

4.3 Kinematics (Fixed 60 Hz Simulation)

Heading 0 = Up the road.
WB = Cab Wheelbase | L = Hitch-to-Axle Length | δ = Front-Wheel Angle (rad)

dθc/dt = (v / WB) · tan(δ)                     [Clamped |θc| ≤ 30° on highway]
Cab Drift: x_c += v · sin(θc) · dt
Hitch Point H = Cab Rear-Axle Vector
dθt/dt = (v / L) · sin(θc − θt)
Trailer Axle Position = H − L · (sin θt, cos θt)  [Hard kinematic constraint]

Note on Feel: Position the trailer axle via strict constraint satisfaction, never direct positional integration, preventing trailer separation under frame hiccups. Tunable trailer_swing_gain (baseline 1.0, testable to 2.5) amplifies visible lag on subtle highway lane maneuvers.

4.4 Default Controls

Mouse steers. Space Throttle, F Brake, W/S Gear shifts (automatic default), Q/E Left/Right blinkers, H Horn. Fully remappable on day one.

5.3 Fair Merge Rules

  • Lane changes are Declared if blinkers run for ≥1.0s (start) before crossing lines; undeclared changes into another car's reaction circle register as Unsafe Lane Change infractions.
  • Merging vehicles spawn offscreen ≥6 car lengths away.
  • Every incoming merge presents a visual warning ≥2.0s prior: headlights, roadside shadows, or visible blinkers.
  • At least one viable escape route (brake clearance, switch, or yield) is guaranteed for 100% of merges.
  • ≥70% of AI merges are signaled. Unsignaled merges require visible lateral drift for ≥0.6s before encroaching.

6. Merge Celebration (The Mario Flag Moment)

Trigger Condition: Evaluated at Merge Gates 1 & 2. The player must hold all criteria continuously for 1.5s (start): centered in mainline lane band, cab and trailer completely past taper, forward speed ≥80% of flow, and zero Contact/Heavy collisions inside the gate envelope.

Merge GradeKinematic Condition at GateCelebration Effect & Payout
Perfect Gap to nearest vehicle ≥1.5× reaction radius, turn signal verified, zero braking forced on traffic. Animation T01 (Full Flourish) + audio sting (≤2.0s) + +$10 + +1 Professional Reputation.
Good Gap ≥1.0× reaction radius, blinker declared. Animation T02 (Reduced Flourish) + +$5.
Messy Meets speed/lane rules but tight gaps; no collision. No screen celebration; short horn tap from neighbor (D11). Payout: $0.
Zero Control Interruption: The player never surrenders steering during the celebration. While the visual overlay flashes, hitting H prompts nearby Safe/Normal drivers to honk back in camaraderie, yielding a +$2 bonus. Rewards pay out once per gate per trip to prevent exploitation.

10. Police & Systemic Traffic Response

10.1 Units & Detection Channels

  • Highway Patrol: 1 active cruiser in Zone 1. Direct vision radius = 6 car lengths.
  • Speed Trap: 1 stationary radar cruiser parked in the S6B bypass.
  • Civilian Witness Calling: Safe/Normal drivers witness collisions or reckless behavior and place CB/phone reports after 20s ± 8s (12–28s window). Pulling over to exchange insurance cancels the call.

10.4 Universal Civilian YIELD Protocol

Triggered whenever cruiser lights activate within 10 car lengths or an active incident occupies the shoulder:

  • Safe Drivers (0.3s reaction): Signal, smoothly drift right, decelerate to 60% flow with hazards active.
  • Normal Drivers (0.5s reaction): Vacate the adjacent lane, moderate brake application.
  • Reckless Drivers (0.4s reaction): Late braking; if pursued directly, 25% roll to bolt and flee.
  • Distracted Drivers (0.9s reaction): Delayed perception; abrupt emergency brake or swerve (D09).
  • Systemic Guarantee (AT-19): Traffic algorithms must guarantee an unblocked path of ≥1.5 truck widths around all shoulder incidents at all times.

15. Notoriety & Economy Tracks

Four independent tracks—never flattened into a single score:

  • Professional Reputation (0–100, starts at 50): Dictates high-paying cargo contracts from Lou.
  • Personal Relationships (−3 to +3 per NPC): Controls prices, repair discounts, and dialogue warmth.
  • Legal Attention (0–100, decays −1/min of clean driving):
    • 0–24: Standard highway patrol baseline.
    • 25–59: Elevated patrol frequency; witness response window drops to 12s.
    • 60–100: Aggressive cruiser pursuit; zero warning leniency; Lou withholds high-profile jobs.
  • Criminal Obligations: Reserved for post-slice expansion.
