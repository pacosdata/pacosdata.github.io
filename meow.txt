## 3) Animations: 30 driving + 50 transitions + safety-radius reaction system
Your plan is excellent. The key is to treat animations as **edges in a behavior graph**, not a pile of clips.

### Core concept: Safety-radius = trigger + context
For each other car, define:
- **perception radius** (they “notice” you)
- **reaction radius** (they “act”)
- **impact radius** (they “panic” / collide)

When your car enters their reaction/impact zone, you don’t just play a generic animation—you play one determined by:
- other driver personality + mood
- relative speed (closing fast vs slow)
- their destination urgency
- your current behavior (braking? swerving? signaling? “aggressive merge”?)

### Animation graph example (collision-adjacent)
When overlap happens:
- Trigger: `enter_reaction_radius`
- Choose animation:
  - Slow/Safe → “apply brakes smoothly” then “lane correct”
  - Reckless → “accelerate + cut away” (more dangerous, bigger risk)
  - Tired → “late brake + wobble” (animation + drift)
  - Hungry/Late → “hard brake or hard pass” (binary)

Then link animations:
- “apply brakes” might transition into:
  - “stop short” if speed low
  - “skid wobble” if speed high
  - “near miss horn” if you clear them
- “lane correct” might transition into:
  - “merge accepted” if you’re already in the right band
  - “side scrape” if timing is bad

This is how you get your “linked to other animations as well as car psychology” requirement.

## 4) Novel collision detection: safety radii + “reaction outcomes”
Instead of “collision if bounding boxes overlap,” do:
- **Proximity events** drive outcomes
- Actual collision is one of several terminal states

Suggested terminal states for the resolver:
1) **Clear** (no contact)
2) **Scrape** (contact light)
3) **Contact** (contact medium)
4) **Spin/Panic** (contact heavy)
5) **Loss of control** (rare, very bad)

Each terminal state then routes to:
- no pull-over (clear)
- horn/gesture + minor notoriety (scrape)
- Papers Please exchange (contact)
- pursuit/impound escalation (heavy)

So your animation system and legal system stay coupled—nice and tidy, meow.

## 5) One practical warning: 50 animations is fine, but keep them parameterized
To avoid an explosion of content, structure animations like:
- **one “brake” animation** with parameters (force, duration, tire skid intensity)
- **one “swerve” family** with 3–5 variants keyed by personality
- transitions driven by state variables rather than unique clips for every tiny case

That way your “50 transitions” become *meaningful* rather than *combinatorial hell*.
